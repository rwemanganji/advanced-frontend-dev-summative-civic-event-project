Accessibility statement

CivicEvents aims to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA so every resident can use city services.

What we do

  • Every page works with a keyboard and has a skip link and a visible focus ring.
  • Text and controls meet AA color contrast. Status is never shown by color alone.
  • Videos have English captions and a transcript. Audio has labeled, keyboard-friendly controls.
  • Animations are reduced when your system asks for reduced motion.

Contact us

If something is hard to use, email help@civicevents.example.
